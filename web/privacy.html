TESR Chat — Privacy Policy

TESR Co., Ltd. (บริษัท ทีอีเอสอาร์ จำกัด), Nonthaburi, Thailand · Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. About this service

TESR Chat is an internal customer-service inbox operated by TESR Co., Ltd. It receives messages that customers send to TESR's official accounts on LINE Official Account, Facebook Messenger and Instagram Direct, so that TESR staff can reply from one place. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we receive

  • Your public profile name and profile picture as provided by the platform (LINE, Facebook, Instagram).
  • A platform-specific user ID (page-scoped / channel-scoped ID).
  • The content of messages you send to TESR, including text, images, videos, files, stickers and locations, with their timestamps.

We do not receive your password, friends list, or any data from conversations that are not with TESR.

3. How we use it

  • To read and reply to your enquiries about TESR Shop, TESR Academy, TESR R&D and TESR Logistics.
  • To keep conversation history so any TESR staff member can continue helping you.
  • To measure our own response times and service quality.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than the infrastructure providers that host the service (Supabase for database/storage, GitHub for the web app) and the messaging platforms themselves.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored in an access-controlled database. Only authorised TESR staff can access it. Conversation data is kept for as long as needed to provide customer service and for business record keeping, or until you ask us to delete it.

5. Data deletion (User data deletion instructions)

You can ask us to delete all data TESR Chat holds about you at any time:

  1. Send a message "ลบข้อมูลของฉัน / Delete my data" to the TESR account you chatted with (LINE, Facebook or Instagram), or email ceo.anoney.potter@gmail.com with the subject "TESR Chat data deletion" and your display name on that platform.
  2. We will delete your profile, messages and attached files from TESR Chat within 30 days and confirm back to you.

You can also remove TESR's access from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, or block/unfollow the TESR account on any platform.

6. Contact

TESR Co., Ltd. · Email: ceo.anoney.potter@gmail.com · Web: tesrshop.com
